About

I study Kinesiology in the School of Education and Human Development at UVA. My coursework so far has covered human anatomy, exercise physiology, and motor learning, and I'm drawn to the questions that sit between the lab and everyday life: why do some people stick with exercise, and what actually helps someone come back from an injury?

Outside of class you'll usually find me at the AFC, on a trail near Grounds, or volunteering at a youth sports clinic. I'm exploring physical therapy and sports medicine as possible paths after graduation.

Interests

  • Injury prevention

    Movement screening, load management, and how small habits keep athletes on the field.

  • Exercise physiology

    How the body adapts to training, from cellular energy systems to whole-body performance.

  • Youth sport

    Making sport a positive, lasting part of kids' lives rather than something they burn out on.

  • Rehabilitation

    The science and the people skills behind helping someone return to what they love doing.
